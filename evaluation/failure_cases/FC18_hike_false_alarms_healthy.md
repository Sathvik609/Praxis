# FC18 — Shoulder-hike flag on every correct rep (REHAB24-6)

**Source:** REHAB24-6 real RGB video + mocap · **Status:** fixed on REHAB24-6; fired often in the 13 Oct team test (FC02)

## What happened
With the fixed 0.08 trunk-length limit, `shoulder_hiking` flagged 47 of 47 correct front-view
reps (and 40 of 41 incorrect ones): healthy shoulder elevation during abduction reaches that limit
by ~90°. On the held-out subjects, 22 of 22 correct reps were flagged.

## Evidence
`evaluation/results/rehab24_v0.md` ("Before and after the 12 Oct thresholds") and
`evaluation/results/rehab24_thresholds_v0.md` ("False alarms on healthy movement: held-out
subjects"), regenerated by `evaluation/scripts/rehab24_eval.py` and `rehab24_thresholds.py`.

## Why
A fixed limit ignores that the shoulder girdle rises normally as the arm goes up.

## What we changed
12 Oct: an angle-aware allowance, the healthy p95 hike per 10° of arm angle fitted on subjects
1, 3, 5, 8, plus 0.05 trunk lengths, held 3 frames (`arm_fsm.py`). Correct reps flagged: 0 of 47;
held-out 0 of 22 (swapped fold 0 of 25).

## Status
Fixed on this dataset, which labels no shrugs, so sensitivity is unmeasured (incorrect reps
flagged fell to 2 of 41). The same allowance still fired 31 times on seated teammates in the 13 Oct
team test (FC02). Since 25 Oct (`b5ecedf`) the flag also needs both hips in view, so a seated
patient whose hips are below a laptop's frame gets no hike flag; not yet re-tested live.
