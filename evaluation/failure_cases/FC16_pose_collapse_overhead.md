# FC16 — Pose skeleton collapsed when the arm went overhead; an off-frame region crashed

**Source:** REHAB24-6 real RGB video (9 healthy adults, lab cameras, mocap reference) · **Status:** fixed

## What happened
Front view, before the fix: pose found on 94.4 % of frames, frame angle MAE 9.97° (95 % limits
−39.8° to 32.6°), rep-peak MAE 16.42°, rep F1 0.74, movement F1 0.38. A pose region wholly
outside the frame raised `cv2.error` in `copyMakeBorder`; it killed the first head-masked run and
would have ended a live session.

## Evidence
`evaluation/results/rehab24_v0_before_pose_fix.md` (code fingerprint `89b5ebc3219c`) and
`evaluation/results/rehab24_v0.md` (after), regenerated by `evaluation/scripts/rehab24_eval.py`.
REHAB24-6 frames are not redistributed (CC BY-NC 4.0).

## Why
The OpenCV Zoo BlazePose port ran the person detector on every frame; with the arm overhead its
mid-hip keypoint landed on the chest and the region collapsed onto the face. It also cropped at
1.0× instead of MediaPipe's 1.25×, cutting off a raised arm.

## What we changed
11 Oct, `services/session/praxis_session/perception/pose.py` (tests in `tests/test_pose.py`):
MediaPipe-style region tracking (the next region comes from the previous landmarks; the detector
runs only when tracking is lost), crop scale 1.25, and an off-frame region now means "no pose".

## Status
Fixed on this dataset: pose found 100 %, frame MAE 3.81°, rep-peak MAE 3.83°, rep F1 0.96
(0.93 before the 25 Oct laptop-framing fix, FC01), movement F1 0.97. Standing adults several metres from a lab camera, not a seated patient at a
laptop; re-check on dataset v1.
