# FC01 — Arm out to the side paused for "hips not in view" on brief dips

**Source:** team test on the hosted 13 Oct build (people + automated) · **Status:** fixed, untested on people

## What happened
The arm view check fired `setup_blocked` 14 times in 9 of 12 Arm out to the side sessions, every
time with `reason: hips_out_of_view`, and paused the set 9 times (a repeat while already paused
does not pause again). Human testers: 4 events in 4 of 6 sessions; automated (`test-claude`): 10
in 5 of 6. In the human sessions it came 3–27 s after the check-in, in two of them mid-set.

## Evidence
- `evaluation/results/team_test_2026-10-21.md`, section "Arm out to the side: where it got in the way" (regenerated by `evaluation/scripts/team_test_report.py`).
- Stored events, e.g. `evaluation/data/team_test_2026-10-21/sessions/c797f70a2c35.json`: event id 5386 `setup_blocked` `hips_out_of_view`, then id 5387 `pause` `rule: setup`, 6.7 s after the check-in (id 5297).
- Commit `dea830d` message (the 21 Oct analysis of the same events).

## Why
The arm angle is shoulder–elbow–hip, so the view gate requires the hip. A seated person at a
laptop camera has the hips at the bottom edge of the frame; small movements drop them out for a
moment, and the gate paused after `SETUP_BLOCK_S` = 3 s.

## What we changed
`dea830d` (21 Oct): `hips_out_of_view` must hold for `HIPS_BLOCK_S` = 6 s before the setup pause;
the other view gates (turned away) keep 3 s. Scoring still freezes from the first gated frame, so
no rep is scored without the hip. `setup_blocked` now carries the visibility numbers, so the next
test can tell a dip from a framing problem. `004009b` relabelled it in the review as "camera view
check paused the set" (the old label said "setup could not see the sheet"; the arm has no sheet).

## Status
Fixed in code and tested offline (`tests/test_pipeline.py`); **not re-tested with people**. The
13 Oct events do not record how long the gate was held, so the 6 s rule cannot be replayed on
these sessions to say how many pauses it would have prevented.
