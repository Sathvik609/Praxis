# FC01 — Arm out to the side paused for "hips not in view" on brief dips

**Source:** team test on the hosted 13 Oct build (people + automated) · **Status:** fixed (25 Oct), measured on laptop-cropped REHAB24-6; not yet re-tested live

## What happened
The arm view check fired `setup_blocked` 14 times in 9 of 12 Arm out to the side sessions, every
time with `reason: hips_out_of_view`, and paused the set 9 times (a repeat while already paused
does not pause again). Human testers: 4 events in 4 of 6 sessions; automated (`test-claude`): 10
in 5 of 6. In the human sessions it came 3–27 s after the check-in, in two of them mid-set.

## Evidence
- `evaluation/results/team_test_2026-10-21.md`, section "Arm out to the side: where it got in the way" (regenerated by `evaluation/scripts/team_test_report.py`).
- Stored events, e.g. `evaluation/data/team_test_2026-10-21/sessions/c797f70a2c35.json`: event id 5386 `setup_blocked` `hips_out_of_view`, then id 5387 `pause` `rule: setup`, 6.7 s after the check-in (id 5297).
- Commit `3dd3c56` message (the 21 Oct analysis of the same events).

## Why
The arm angle is shoulder–elbow–hip, so the view gate requires the hip. A seated person at a
laptop camera has the hips at the bottom edge of the frame; small movements drop them out for a
moment, and the gate paused after `SETUP_BLOCK_S` = 3 s.

## What we changed
`3dd3c56` (21 Oct): `hips_out_of_view` must hold for `HIPS_BLOCK_S` = 6 s before the setup pause;
the other view gates (turned away) keep 3 s. Scoring still freezes from the first gated frame, so
no rep is scored without the hip. `setup_blocked` now carries the visibility numbers, so the next
test can tell a dip from a framing problem. `ffc6341` relabelled it in the review as "camera view
check paused the set" (the old label said "setup could not see the sheet"; the arm has no sheet).

The 6 s wait did not reach the real problem: seated at a laptop, the hips are at or below the
bottom of the frame for the whole set, not just for a moment. A later live session (23 Oct, read
by hand from `/api/review`, not in a committed file; `docs/TECHNICAL_REPORT.md` §8.4) counted 16
reps with 0 verified.

## 25 Oct: the front view no longer needs the hips
`2d991a8` and `f91a0e6` (25 Oct): the front view now gates on the practising shoulder and elbow
only (BlazePose still places the hips just below the frame well enough for the angle); a dip back
under the start line within 0.8 s is not a rep; the shoulder-hike flag needs both hips, since it
is measured in trunk lengths; the setup check and the pause hint no longer ask for the hips.
Tests in `tests/test_arm_fsm.py`, `tests/test_pipeline.py` and
`tests/test_agent_active_perception.py`.

Measured on REHAB24-6 cropped to laptop framing (4 front-view recordings, 35 reps;
`evaluation/results/rehab24_laptop_framing_v0.md`, regenerated by
`evaluation/scripts/rehab24_laptop_framing.py`):

| framing | rep F1 before → after | reps found before → after | setup pauses before → after |
|---|---|---|---|
| hips at the bottom edge | 0.76 → 0.93 | 24 → 34 | 2 → 0 |
| hips out of frame | 0.28 → 0.91 | 6 → 35 | 11 → 1 |

Angle MAE with the hips out of frame is 7.5° (3.5° with the full body in view). On the full
REHAB24-6 front view the angle MAE is unchanged at 3.81° and rep F1 went from 0.93 to 0.96
(`evaluation/results/rehab24_v0.md`). These are healthy adults standing in a lab, cropped to look
like a laptop view; they are not seated patients.

## Status
Fixed (25 Oct), measured on laptop-cropped REHAB24-6; **not yet re-tested live**. The 13 Oct
events do not record how long the gate was held, so neither fix can be replayed on these sessions
to say how many pauses it would have prevented.
