# FC12 — Hand model finds the rendered hand on 3–9 % of frames; pegs unverified

**Source:** synthetic 3-D renders (**not real-camera evidence**) · **Status:** open (render limit; real-hand rate unmeasured)

## What happened
The OpenCV Zoo palm-detection + hand-landmark models found a hand on 3.1 % of cup frames and
8.9 % of NHPT frames. Of 91 NHPT reports, 5 were verified and 86 unverified (`grasp_not_seen` 45,
`no_hand_near_hole` 41); verified agreement on matched pegs was 0.065. Most inferred timer starts
miss the first transfer (≈1.5 s).

## Evidence
`evaluation/results/synthetic3d_v0.md` ("Unverified reasons and coverage", "hand model frames"
column), regenerated by `evaluation/scripts/eval_3d.py`.

## Why
The rendered hand is a primitive proxy (palm box, capsule fingers), which the palm detector
rarely accepts. A peg needs the pinch (grasp) and the hand near the hole to be verified, so with
no hand nearly every seat is counted but unverified.

## What we changed
12 Oct: a seat whose grasp was not seen is counted `unverified (grasp_not_seen)` instead of being
dropped (before, a missed pinch meant the test never started), and an inferred timer start is
labelled. No hand threshold was loosened to raise a number.

## Status
Open. The renders say nothing about real hands; measure the hand model's detection rate on real
laptop-camera footage (dataset v1) before tuning. `docs/RISKS.md` (2026-10-12).
