# FC11 — Waiter – cup: the cup was almost never found

**Source:** synthetic 3-D renders (**not real-camera evidence**) · **Status:** fixed (renders only); reach-time bias open

## What happened
On 6 rendered cup clips (192 reaches) only 23 were reported, all in one clip: rep F1 0.21
(P 1.0 / R 0.12); five clips found no reach at all.

## Evidence
`evaluation/results/synthetic3d_v0_before_fix.md` and `evaluation/results/synthetic3d_v0.md`
(per-clip rows `cup3d_01`–`06`), regenerated by `evaluation/scripts/eval_3d.py`.

## Why
The cup is found by its blue tape ring. From a laptop camera the ring is a vertical band whose
near half shows as a crescent, smaller than the 30 mm disc the detector expected.

## What we changed
12 Oct (`services/session/tests/test_cup3d.py`): a circle is fitted to the crescent's
camera-side edge, which is the cup's bottom edge lying on the paper, giving the base centre; the
session learns the cup's radius from tight fits and uses partly hidden bands for the centre only.

## Status
Fixed on the renders: rep F1 0.80 (P 1.0 / R 0.67), 127 verified / 2 unverified (`marker_lost`).
Reach times still run long (MAE 773 ms, bias +760 ms) because the gripping hand hides the band at
grasp and release. The remaining misses are FC13 and FC14.
