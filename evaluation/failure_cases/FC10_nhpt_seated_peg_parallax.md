# FC10 — NHPT: a seated peg read empty and became a phantom free peg

**Source:** synthetic 3-D renders (Blender, laptop-camera angle; **not real-camera evidence**) · **Status:** fixed (renders only)

## What happened
On 10 rendered NHPT clips (90 pegs) the board state missed most seats: rep F1 0.41 (P 0.58 /
R 0.31), 28 TP, 20 FP, 62 FN, only 2 of 10 clips completed the timer.

## Evidence
`evaluation/results/synthetic3d_v0_before_fix.md` (before, perception code at `38b7ec9`) and
`evaluation/results/synthetic3d_v0.md` (after, same clips and settings), regenerated by
`evaluation/scripts/eval_3d.py`; cached events in `evaluation/dataset_3d_v0/nhpt3d_*.pred.json`.

## Why
A real 32 mm peg stands in a board. From 20–50° of camera tilt it projects onto the sheet plane
as a streak that starts past its hole and ends 10–40 mm away. Read on the hole disc alone, a
seated peg looked empty, its streak was taken for a free peg (phantom grasps and drops), and at
~50° it lit the next row's hole.

## What we changed
12 Oct, test-first (`services/session/tests/test_parallax.py`): occupancy looks first at a seat
column per hole, the hole raised 10–24 mm and projected with `cv2.projectPoints` through a camera
whose focal comes from a single-view `cv2.calibrateCamera` on the 16 marker corners; free pegs
exclude every hole's footprint. No threshold was loosened.

## Status
Fixed on the renders: rep F1 0.85 (P 0.85 / R 0.86), 9 of 10 clips complete, NHPT total MAE
2.6 s. Assumes the standard 32 mm peg and a board up to ~12 mm; never seen a real peg or board.
Remaining extras: FC15.
