# FC17 — Half-profile reads ~12° low; no pose without the head in frame

**Source:** REHAB24-6 real RGB video · **Status:** open

## What happened
- Half-profile (~45° turn): the front-view gate opens on 34.4 % of frames, and where it opens the angle reads low: frame MAE 12.19°, bias −11.59°, rep-peak MAE 15.09°, rep F1 0.60.
- Head out of frame (front view, subjects 2, 4, 8): with a grey box over the head a pose was found on 51.1 % of frames and was mostly wrong (frame MAE 25.97°, rep F1 0.41); with the head cropped out, on 0 %.

## Evidence
`evaluation/results/rehab24_v0.md` ("Headline by camera view", "Does the head need to be
visible?") and `evaluation/results/rehab24_thresholds_v0.md` §2 (error vs turn), regenerated by
`evaluation/scripts/rehab24_eval.py` / `rehab24_thresholds.py`.

## Why
The angle is a 2-D, camera-relative number: turning away foreshortens the arm. The front-view gate
was moved from 0.6 to 0.55 trunk lengths on 12 Oct so a slightly turned patient is not told to
turn; the price is that more half-turned frames are scored, and they read low. The MediaPipe
person detector anchors on the face, so the pose stage needs the head even though Praxis never
reads the face.

## Why we did not change more
No yaw-labelled data of seated patients. For the head, the copy was changed instead: since
11 Oct (`01ebe66`) the camera-setup placement text for Arm out to the side asks for the head in
view ("Your face is never analysed, but the camera finds you by your head",
`web/src/screens/05-camera-setup/SetupDiagram.tsx`), and so do the person hint and the live status
line; the setup checklist's pose row fails when no pose is found.

## Status
Open. Possibly involved in FC03 (low peaks), unproven.
